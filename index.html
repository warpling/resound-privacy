Resound Privacy Policy

Last updated November 6, 2026

Resound is an iPhone app for sharing songs with friends. It is made by Shapes and Stories LLC ("we"). This policy explains what the app stores and why.

What Resound stores

Resound keeps its data in Apple's iCloud (CloudKit), in a database that everyone using the app can read. What you put there is visible to other Resound users:

  • Your profile: the display name you choose and an optional bio.
  • The songs you post or repost, with any note you add, and the song's title, artist, album, artwork and music-service IDs.
  • Your likes, comments and follows.
  • Listens: a record that you played a post, used to rank trending songs.

Signing in

You sign in with Apple. Resound asks only for your name, to suggest a display name, and does not ask for your email address. Your account is identified by an anonymous iCloud user ID.

Spotify

If you connect Spotify, Resound uses Spotify's API to search for songs, play them on your Spotify app or devices, add them to your queue, and read what is playing and what you played recently. The Spotify sign-in token is kept in your iPhone's Keychain and is never uploaded to iCloud or to us. Spotify's own privacy policy covers what Spotify does with your data.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, iCloud sends them when someone follows you, or likes, comments on or reposts your posts.

What Resound does not do

  • No ads, analytics, tracking or third-party SDKs that collect data.
  • No selling or sharing of your data with anyone.
  • No access to your contacts, location or photo library.

Deleting your data

You can delete your posts in the app. To have everything removed, including your profile, comments, likes, follows and listens, email us and we will delete it.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top of this page.
